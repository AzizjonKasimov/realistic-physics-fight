# Changelog

Each version of the Fight page, newest first. The page shows the newest
entry's lines as written.

## v0.1.1 (2026-10-19)

- Punches thrown at nearly the same moment no longer crush the fighters' arms: the guards bump and both punches come back.
- The fighters' feet stay on the floor when they punch or lean back, instead of a leg swinging out.

## v0.1 (2026-10-19)

- Fight: you play the left fighter. Punch with D or the right arrow, and hold A or the left arrow to lean back so a punch falls short.
- The right fighter spars on its own, throwing a punch every few seconds, until a friend takes it over with J and L.
- On a phone, tap PUNCH and hold LEAN BACK; a friend can take the buttons on the right.
- Nobody wins yet: blows hurt the fighters as they hurt bodies in the sandbox, and R starts the fight afresh.
