Fight

Two boxers seen from the side, on a realistic injury simulation: step in, jab, cross, block, duck, and lean back against a computer opponent, over three one-minute rounds. Every blow hurts skin, muscle and bone through real physics, and a hard one to the jaw can knock a fighter out.

Content warning: graphic simulated injury, including blood, torn tissue and broken bones.

About

A fight is three rounds of a minute. A knockout or a fall ends it at once, as nobody gets up yet; otherwise the fighter who landed more clean punches, on the head or the body rather than the arms, wins. Punching opens your guard until the punch is back. The opponent sees what you do a moment late, as a person would, and makes mistakes. R starts the next fight. Fights decided by their other injuries come next.
