Fight

Two fighters on a realistic injury simulation: punch, lean back out of a punch's way, and every blow hurts skin, muscle and bone through real physics.

Content warning: graphic simulated injury, including blood, torn tissue and broken bones.

D or →
Punch the other fighter
A or ←
Hold to lean back, so a punch falls short
J / L

Space / R / M

Touch

About

Nobody wins yet: the fight goes on until you start it afresh. Deciding fights by their injuries, a computer opponent, and rounds come next.

Made by Azizjon Kasimov with AI coding agents, on the engine of the Realistic Physics sandbox.
